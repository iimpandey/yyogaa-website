THE YYOGAA APPROACH

Listen without forcing.

01

Get comfortable

Create a position where your body can settle without unnecessary tension.

02

Notice sound

Let tones, silence and vibration come and go without chasing them.

03

Stay curious

Different sounds may feel different from one session to the next.

04

Rest afterward

Give yourself a quiet moment before immediately returning to activity.

LISTEN

Let sound create space.

No performance. No perfect technique. Just attention, rest and listening.
