THE YYOGAA APPROACH

Breathe simply.

01

Notice first

Begin by observing your natural breathing pattern without judgment.

02

Stay gentle

Breathwork does not need to feel forceful to be meaningful.

03

Follow your body

Stop or return to normal breathing if a practice feels uncomfortable.

04

Practice often

A few mindful minutes each day can build familiarity and awareness.

RETURN

Come back to your breath.

You don't need perfect conditions. You only need one conscious breath to begin.
