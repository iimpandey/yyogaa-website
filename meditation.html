Meditation is not about stopping your thoughts.

Meditation is the practice of noticing what is happening without needing to control every thought or feeling.

Yyogaa keeps meditation simple, practical and approachable so you can build a practice that fits into everyday life.

THE YYOGAA APPROACH

Keep it simple.

01

Start small

Five focused minutes can be enough to begin building the habit.

02

Notice

Pay attention to thoughts, breath and sensation without forcing change.

03

Return gently

When your mind wanders, simply return to your chosen point of focus.

04

Practice consistently

Regular short sessions can help meditation feel more natural over time.
