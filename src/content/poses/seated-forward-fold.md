name: Seated Forward Fold
sanskrit:
  iast: Paścimottānāsana
  simple: Paschimottanasana
alsoKnownAs: [Seated Forward Bend]
summary: A seated pose that folds gently forward over straight or bent legs, lengthening the back of the body without forcing.

level: beginner
category: forward-fold
alsoIn: [seated]
bodyFocus: [hamstrings, spine]
goals: [calm, flexibility]
props: [mat, blanket, strap]

sided: false
holdGuide: 5–10 slow breaths.
setup: Sit on your mat with your legs stretched out in front of you. If your lower back rounds, sit on the edge of a folded blanket or cushion and bend your knees.
steps:
  - Sit tall, with your feet hip-width apart and your toes pointing up.
  - Bend your knees as much as you need to keep your back long.
  - As you breathe in, lengthen up through your spine.
  - As you breathe out, fold forward from your hips, leading with your chest rather than your head.
  - Rest your hands wherever they reach comfortably, on your thighs, shins or ankles, or hold a strap looped around your feet.
  - Stop where you feel a gentle stretch, not pain, and let your neck relax.
  - Stay for a few breaths, easing a little further only if it feels comfortable.
breathCue: Breathe in to lengthen your spine. Breathe out to soften, without pulling yourself further forward.
comingOut: Breathe in and slowly roll up to sitting, bringing your head up last. Bend your knees and rest.

benefits:
  - Stretches the backs of the legs
  - Invites length through the back
  - Many people find the quiet, folded shape settling

commonMistakes:
  - mistake: Pulling yourself forward with your arms
    fix: Let your arms rest, and let your breath and time do the work.
  - mistake: Rounding the lower back to reach the toes
    fix: Sit on a folded blanket and bend your knees. Reaching your toes is not the aim.
  - mistake: Locking the knees straight
    fix: Keep a soft bend in your knees throughout.

modifications:
  - Sit on a folded blanket or cushion so your pelvis can tip forward more easily.
  - Bend your knees generously, even resting your chest on your thighs.
  - Loop a strap or scarf around the balls of your feet and hold it with both hands.
  - Rest your forehead on a bolster or cushion placed along your legs.

In practice, the fold in this pose is often small. A long back with bent knees is a complete version of Seated Forward Fold.

Let it be an unhurried pose. Forward folds tend to open slowly, over many breaths and many practices.
