name: Happy Baby Pose
sanskrit:
  iast: Ānanda Bālāsana
  simple: Ananda Balasana
alsoKnownAs: [Happy Baby]
summary: A playful pose lying on your back with your knees drawn wide towards your armpits, gently opening the hips and resting the back.

level: beginner
category: hip-opener
alsoIn: [supine]
bodyFocus: [hips, spine, hamstrings]
goals: [calm, mobility, recovery]
props: [mat, strap]

sided: false
holdGuide: 5–10 slow breaths, or as long as it feels comfortable.
setup: Lie on your back on your mat. If your chin lifts, rest your head on a thin pillow or folded blanket. Keep a strap nearby.
steps:
  - Draw your knees in towards your chest.
  - Let your knees open a little wider than your body, moving towards your armpits.
  - Lift your feet so your shins point up towards the ceiling, with the soles of your feet facing up.
  - Hold your shins, the backs of your thighs, or the outer edges of your feet, whichever you can reach comfortably. A strap around each foot works too.
  - Keep your head and shoulders resting on the mat.
  - Let your lower back soften towards the floor. If you like, rock gently from side to side.
  - Stay for a few slow breaths.
breathCue: Breathe in and feel your belly rise. Breathe out and let your hips and lower back soften.
comingOut: Release your hands, bring your knees together and place your feet on the mat. Rest for a breath before moving on.

benefits:
  - Gently opens the hips and inner thighs
  - Lets the lower back rest against the floor
  - Many people find it calming and a little playful

commonMistakes:
  - mistake: Lifting the head and shoulders to reach the feet
    fix: Hold your shins or thighs instead, or use a strap, so your head can stay down.
  - mistake: Pulling the knees down hard
    fix: Let your knees draw down gently with the weight of your arms, without forcing.
  - mistake: Lifting the tailbone high off the mat
    fix: Ease your grip so your lower back and tailbone can soften towards the floor.

modifications:
  - Hold the backs of your thighs or your shins instead of your feet.
  - Loop a strap around each foot and hold the ends.
  - Hold one leg at a time, keeping the other foot on the floor.
  - Rest your head on a thin pillow or folded blanket.

Happy Baby is one of the more playful poses. It is fine to rock, wiggle, or smile a little.

Where you hold does not matter. Shins, thighs or a strap all let you rest in the same easy shape.
