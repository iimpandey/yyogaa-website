name: Low Lunge
sanskrit:
  iast: Añjaneyāsana
  simple: Anjaneyasana
alsoKnownAs: [Kneeling Lunge]
summary: A kneeling lunge with one foot forwards and the back knee resting on the mat, gently stretching the front of the back hip and thigh.

level: beginner
category: hip-opener
alsoIn: []
bodyFocus: [hips, legs]
goals: [flexibility, mobility]
props: [mat, blanket, block]

sided: true
holdGuide: 3–6 slow breaths on each side.
setup: Come onto your hands and knees. Fold a blanket and place it where your back knee will rest. Keep two blocks nearby.
steps:
  - Step your right foot forwards between your hands, so your right knee is roughly over your right ankle.
  - Slide your left knee back a little and rest it on the folded blanket, with the top of your left foot on the mat.
  - Rest your hands on the floor or on blocks on either side of your front foot.
  - Keep your right knee pointing the same way as your toes.
  - As you breathe out, let your hips ease gently forwards, only until you feel a mild stretch at the front of your left hip.
  - If you feel steady, bring your hands to your front thigh or raise your arms and lift your chest.
  - Stay for a few breaths, then come out and repeat on the other side.
breathCue: Breathe in to lift and lengthen through your chest. Breathe out to let your hips soften a little forwards.
comingOut: Bring your hands back to the floor or blocks, ease your hips back and step your front foot back to hands and knees. Rest for a breath, then practise the second side.

benefits:
  - Stretches the front of the back hip and thigh
  - Gently opens the hips
  - Builds steadiness in the legs

commonMistakes:
  - mistake: Kneeling directly on a hard floor
    fix: Pad your back knee with a folded blanket or the doubled-over end of your mat.
  - mistake: Letting the front knee move far past the ankle
    fix: Step your front foot further forwards so your knee stays roughly over your ankle.
  - mistake: Pushing the hips forwards too far
    fix: Ease forwards only until you feel a mild stretch, and keep your breath easy.
  - mistake: Arching the lower back
    fix: Draw your belly gently in and keep your hands on blocks or your thigh.

modifications:
  - Keep your hands on blocks at their highest height.
  - Place a folded blanket under your back knee, or fold your mat over for extra padding.
  - Practise next to a wall or a sturdy chair so you can steady yourself with one hand.
  - Keep your hips back over your knee for a gentler stretch.

Low Lunge is a kind way to open the front of the hips, especially after a lot of sitting. The stretch is often felt more than seen.

Small movements count. Easing your hips forwards just a little can be plenty.
