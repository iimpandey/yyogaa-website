name: Easy Pose
sanskrit:
  iast: Sukhāsana
  simple: Sukhasana
alsoKnownAs: [Easy Seat, Cross-Legged Pose]
summary: A simple cross-legged seat with a tall, relaxed spine, often used for breathing practice, meditation, or a quiet start to practice.

level: beginner
category: seated
alsoIn: []
bodyFocus: [hips, spine]
goals: [calm, focus, posture, beginner-friendly]
props: [mat, blanket]

sided: false
holdGuide: 1–5 minutes, or as long as you can sit comfortably.
setup: Fold a blanket and place it at the back of your mat. Sit on the edge of it so your hips are a little higher than your knees.
steps:
  - Cross your shins in front of you, letting each foot rest under the opposite knee or shin.
  - Let your knees relax down towards the floor. They do not need to touch it.
  - Rest your hands on your thighs or knees, palms up or down, whichever feels easier.
  - Lengthen up through your spine, as if the crown of your head were gently rising.
  - Let your shoulders relax and your chin rest level.
  - Close your eyes, or rest your gaze softly on the floor in front of you.
  - Stay for as long as you are comfortable. Next time, cross your legs the other way.
breathCue: Let your breath be slow and natural. Notice it moving in and out without trying to change it.
comingOut: Lean forwards onto your hands, uncross your legs and stretch them out in front of you. Gently shake out your legs before moving on.

benefits:
  - Offers a steady, simple seat for breathing practice and meditation
  - Encourages a tall, easy posture
  - Gently opens the hips
  - Many people find it calming and grounding

commonMistakes:
  - mistake: Sitting flat on the floor with the knees higher than the hips
    fix: Sit on a folded blanket or cushion so your hips are above your knees.
  - mistake: Slumping or leaning back
    fix: Raise your seat a little higher, or sit with your back against a wall.
  - mistake: Always crossing the same leg in front
    fix: Switch which shin is in front each time you sit.
  - mistake: Staying when the legs go numb
    fix: Uncross your legs and stretch them out. Shorter sits are fine.

modifications:
  - Sit on a higher stack of folded blankets or a firm cushion.
  - Sit with your back against a wall for support.
  - Sit on a sturdy chair with your feet flat on the floor and your back tall.
  - Place blankets or cushions under your knees if they feel strained.

Easy Pose is not always easy at first. Sitting on the floor can feel unfamiliar, and a simple blanket under your hips often makes a big difference.

The aim is a seat you can stay in comfortably while you breathe. A chair counts too.
