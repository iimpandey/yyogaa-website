name: Bound Angle Pose
sanskrit:
  iast: Baddha Koṇāsana
  simple: Baddha Konasana
alsoKnownAs: [Butterfly Pose, Cobbler's Pose]
summary: A seated pose with the soles of the feet together and the knees falling open to the sides, gently opening the hips and inner thighs.

level: beginner
category: hip-opener
alsoIn: [seated]
bodyFocus: [hips, legs, spine]
goals: [flexibility, mobility, calm]
props: [mat, blanket, block]

sided: false
holdGuide: 5–10 slow breaths, or a little longer if it feels comfortable.
setup: Sit on the edge of a folded blanket with your legs stretched out in front of you. Keep two blocks or cushions nearby.
steps:
  - Bend your knees and bring the soles of your feet together, letting your knees fall open to the sides.
  - Slide your feet a comfortable distance away from you. Further away usually feels easier on the hips and knees.
  - If your knees hover high, rest each one on a block or cushion.
  - Hold your ankles or shins, or rest your hands on the floor beside you.
  - Lengthen up through your spine and let your shoulders relax.
  - Let your knees relax down only as far as they go on their own. Never press them down with your hands.
  - Stay for a few slow breaths, sitting tall.
breathCue: Breathe in to sit tall. Breathe out to let your hips and inner thighs soften, without pushing.
comingOut: Use your hands to bring your knees gently together, then stretch your legs out in front of you and rest for a breath.

benefits:
  - Gently stretches the inner thighs and hips
  - Encourages a tall, easy seat
  - Many people find it a calm, settling pose

commonMistakes:
  - mistake: Pushing the knees down with the hands
    fix: Let your knees fall only as far as they go on their own, and support them with blocks or cushions.
  - mistake: Pulling the feet in very close to the body
    fix: Slide your feet further away until your hips and knees feel at ease.
  - mistake: Slumping the back
    fix: Sit on a higher folded blanket and lengthen up through your spine.

modifications:
  - Place blocks or cushions under your knees.
  - Sit on one or more folded blankets to raise your hips.
  - Sit with your back against a wall for support.
  - Lie on your back with the soles of your feet together and cushions under your knees for a reclining version.

Bound Angle Pose can be a quiet place to sit and breathe. The height of your knees says nothing about how well you are doing.

Support is welcome here. With blocks or cushions under your knees, the muscles of your hips and thighs can let go a little more.
