name: Supine Twist
sanskrit:
  iast: Supta Matsyendrāsana
  simple: Supta Matsyendrasana
alsoKnownAs: [Reclining Twist, Lying Twist]
summary: A gentle twist lying on your back, letting your bent knees fall to one side while both shoulders rest heavy on the mat.

level: beginner
category: twist
alsoIn: [supine]
bodyFocus: [spine, hips, chest]
goals: [calm, mobility, recovery]
props: [mat, blanket]

sided: true
holdGuide: 5–10 slow breaths on each side.
setup: Lie on your back with your knees bent and your feet flat on the mat. Keep a folded blanket or cushion nearby.
steps:
  - Stretch your arms out to the sides at shoulder height, palms facing up or down.
  - Lift your feet and draw your knees in over your hips, keeping them stacked together.
  - As you breathe out, slowly let both knees fall over to the right, only as far as feels easy.
  - If your knees do not reach the floor, rest them on a folded blanket or cushion.
  - Keep both shoulders heavy on the mat. If your left shoulder lifts, let your knees come up a little.
  - Turn your head to look up, or gently to the left if that feels comfortable on your neck.
  - Stay for a few breaths, then bring your knees back to the centre and repeat on the other side.
breathCue: Breathe in and feel your ribs widen. Breathe out and let your body rest a little more heavily on the floor.
comingOut: As you breathe in, draw your knees back up over your hips, using your hands to help if you like. Place your feet on the mat and rest for a breath, then practise the second side.

benefits:
  - Invites gentle movement through the spine
  - Stretches the outer hips and the front of the chest
  - Many people find it a soothing way to wind down

commonMistakes:
  - mistake: Letting the top shoulder lift off the mat
    fix: Bring your knees up higher or rest them on a cushion, so both shoulders can stay down.
  - mistake: Forcing the knees to the floor
    fix: Support your knees with a blanket or cushion and keep the twist small.
  - mistake: Turning the head too far
    fix: Keep your gaze up towards the ceiling if your neck feels any strain.

modifications:
  - Rest your knees on a folded blanket, cushion or bolster.
  - Place a cushion between your knees to keep them comfortable.
  - Keep your feet on the floor and let your knees tip only a little to the side.
  - Rest one hand on your belly and the other on your chest if your shoulders are uncomfortable with the arms out.

Supine Twist is often practised near the end of a session. Letting the floor hold you makes it a restful kind of twist.

There is no need to go deep. A small, easy turn, with plenty of support, is usually the most comfortable.
