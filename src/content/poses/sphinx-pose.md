name: Sphinx Pose
sanskrit:
  iast: Sālamba Bhujaṅgāsana
  simple: Salamba Bhujangasana
alsoKnownAs: [Sphinx]
summary: A gentle backbend lying on your front, propped up on your forearms, with the chest lifted a little and the legs resting on the mat.

level: beginner
category: prone
alsoIn: [backbend]
bodyFocus: [spine, chest, shoulders]
goals: [mobility, posture, calm]
props: [mat, blanket]

sided: false
holdGuide: 5–10 slow breaths, then lower and rest. Repeat once if it feels good.
setup: Lie on your front with your legs hip-width apart and the tops of your feet resting on the mat. Rest your forehead on your hands for a moment.
steps:
  - Place your elbows under your shoulders, or slightly in front of them, with your forearms resting on the mat.
  - Spread your hands flat, with your forearms parallel or your hands gently together.
  - As you breathe in, slowly lift your head and chest, letting your forearms take some of the weight.
  - Keep the lift low and easy, with the front of your hips resting on the mat.
  - Draw your shoulders gently back and away from your ears.
  - Keep the back of your neck long, with your gaze down or slightly forward.
  - Stay for a few breaths, keeping the pose comfortable in your lower back.
breathCue: Breathe in and feel your chest widen. Breathe out to soften your shoulders and let your legs rest heavy.
comingOut: Slide your elbows forwards and slowly lower your chest and forehead to the mat. Turn your head to one side and rest, or press back into Child's Pose.

benefits:
  - Invites gentle movement into the upper and middle back
  - Opens across the front of the chest and shoulders
  - Offers a supported, easy way to explore a backbend

commonMistakes:
  - mistake: Placing the elbows too far behind the shoulders
    fix: Move your elbows under your shoulders or slightly forwards, which makes the pose gentler.
  - mistake: Sinking into the shoulders
    fix: Press your forearms gently into the mat and lift your chest a little away from the floor.
  - mistake: Lifting the chin and squeezing the back of the neck
    fix: Keep your chin slightly down and your neck long.

modifications:
  - Move your elbows further forwards for a lower, gentler lift.
  - Place a folded blanket under your hips or forearms if lying on your front is uncomfortable.
  - Rest your forehead on your stacked hands for a break between rounds.

Sphinx is a gentle way to meet a backbend. Resting on your forearms means you can stay a little longer and simply breathe.

If you feel any pinching in your lower back, move your elbows forwards and come lower.
