name: Knees-to-Chest Pose
sanskrit:
  iast: Apānāsana
  simple: Apanasana
alsoKnownAs: [Knees to Chest]
summary: A simple, restful pose lying on your back and hugging your knees towards your chest, letting the lower back soften into the floor.

level: beginner
category: supine
alsoIn: []
bodyFocus: [spine, hips, glutes]
goals: [calm, recovery, beginner-friendly]
props: [mat]

sided: false
holdGuide: 5–10 slow breaths, or as long as feels comfortable.
setup: Lie on your back on your mat with your knees bent and your feet flat on the floor.
steps:
  - Lift one foot and then the other, drawing your knees in towards your chest.
  - Rest your hands on your shins, or hold the backs of your thighs if that is easier on your knees.
  - Let your knees be a little apart or together, whichever feels better.
  - Keep your head and shoulders resting on the mat, with your neck relaxed.
  - Let your lower back soften towards the floor.
  - If you like, rock gently from side to side, or draw small circles with your knees.
  - Stay for a few slow breaths.
breathCue: Breathe in and let your knees move slightly away as your belly rises. Breathe out and draw them gently back in.
comingOut: Release your hands and place your feet back on the mat one at a time. Rest for a breath before moving on.

benefits:
  - Lets the lower back rest and soften
  - Gently stretches the buttocks and the backs of the hips
  - Many people find it calming and comforting

commonMistakes:
  - mistake: Pulling the knees in hard
    fix: Hold lightly and let your knees come only as close as is comfortable.
  - mistake: Lifting the head and shoulders off the mat
    fix: Hold the backs of your thighs instead, so your head can stay resting down.
  - mistake: Holding the breath
    fix: Let the breath move freely, letting your knees rise and fall with it.

modifications:
  - Hug one knee at a time, keeping the other foot on the floor or the leg stretched out.
  - Hold the backs of your thighs instead of your shins.
  - Rest your head on a thin folded blanket.

Knees-to-Chest Pose is one of the simplest shapes in yoga. It can be a gentle way to start a practice lying down, or a place to rest between other poses.

Let it be easy. A light hold and a slow breath are all it asks for.
