name: Triangle Pose
sanskrit:
  iast: Utthita Trikoṇāsana
  simple: Utthita Trikonasana
alsoKnownAs: [Triangle, Extended Triangle Pose]
summary: A wide-legged standing pose that reaches the body out over the front leg, lengthening both sides of the waist and the backs of the legs.

level: beginner
category: standing
alsoIn: []
bodyFocus: [legs, hamstrings, side-body, hips]
goals: [flexibility, strength, focus]
props: [mat, block]

sided: true
holdGuide: 3–5 slow breaths on each side to begin with.
setup: Stand facing the long edge of your mat and step your feet wide apart, a little less wide than for Warrior II. Keep a block nearby.
steps:
  - Turn your right foot out so your toes point to the short end of the mat, and angle your left toes slightly inwards.
  - Raise your arms out to the sides at shoulder height, with your palms facing down.
  - Keep your right knee soft rather than locked, and keep both legs gently active.
  - As you breathe out, reach your body out over your right leg, folding from the hip and keeping both sides of your waist long.
  - Rest your right hand on your shin, on a block, or on the floor outside your foot. Never press your hand directly on your knee.
  - Reach your left arm up towards the ceiling, or rest your left hand on your hip.
  - Look down, forwards or up at your top hand, whichever feels easy on your neck. Stay for a few breaths, then repeat on the other side.
breathCue: Breathe in to lengthen from your feet to the crown of your head. Breathe out to let your chest open gently towards the side.
comingOut: Bend your front knee a little, press into your feet and, as you breathe in, rise back up to standing. Turn your feet to face the long edge of the mat, then practise the second side.

benefits:
  - Stretches the backs of the legs and the sides of the waist
  - Builds steady strength in the legs
  - Invites the chest and shoulders to open
  - Encourages balance and focus

commonMistakes:
  - mistake: Pressing the hand onto the front knee
    fix: Move your hand to your shin, a block, or your thigh above the knee, so the joint is not pushed.
  - mistake: Reaching down so far that the waist collapses
    fix: Come up a little higher and rest your hand on a block, keeping both sides of your waist equally long.
  - mistake: Locking the front knee
    fix: Keep a tiny bend in your front knee, so the leg is active without pushing backwards.
  - mistake: Straining the neck to look up
    fix: Look down at the mat or straight ahead instead.

modifications:
  - Rest your lower hand on a block, on any height, outside or in front of your front foot.
  - Keep your top hand on your hip rather than reaching it up.
  - Take a shorter stance to feel steadier.
  - Practise with your back against a wall to help you keep the body in one flat plane.

Triangle is about length more than depth. A hand resting high on a block, with both sides of the waist long, is a full and complete version of the pose.

Take your time setting up your feet. A steady base makes the rest of the shape feel much easier.
