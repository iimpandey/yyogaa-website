name: Cobra Pose
sanskrit:
  iast: Bhujaṅgāsana
  simple: Bhujangasana
alsoKnownAs: [Cobra]
summary: A gentle backbend lying on your front, lifting the chest a little away from the floor while the legs and hips stay grounded.

level: beginner
category: backbend
alsoIn: [prone]
bodyFocus: [spine, chest, shoulders]
goals: [mobility, posture, energy]
props: [mat]

sided: false
holdGuide: 2–5 slow breaths, then lower and rest. Repeat once or twice if it feels good.
setup: Lie on your front with your legs hip-width apart and the tops of your feet resting on the mat. Place your hands under your shoulders, with your elbows close to your sides.
steps:
  - Rest your forehead on the mat and press the tops of your feet and the front of your hips gently into the floor.
  - Draw your shoulders gently back and away from your ears.
  - As you breathe in, slowly lift your head and chest a little way off the mat, using the muscles of your back more than your arms.
  - Keep your elbows bent and close to your body, with your lower ribs near the mat.
  - Keep the back of your neck long, with your gaze down or slightly forward.
  - Stay for a few breaths, lifting only as high as feels comfortable in your lower back.
breathCue: Breathe in as you lift. Breathe out to soften your shoulders, staying at a height that lets you breathe easily.
comingOut: Breathe out and slowly lower your chest and forehead to the mat. Turn your head to one side and rest, or press back into Child's Pose.

benefits:
  - Invites gentle movement into the upper and middle back
  - Opens across the front of the chest and shoulders
  - Builds strength in the muscles along the back

commonMistakes:
  - mistake: Pushing up high with straight arms
    fix: Lift only as far as your back muscles take you, keeping your elbows bent.
  - mistake: Squeezing the back of the neck
    fix: Keep your chin slightly down and your neck long.
  - mistake: Lifting the shoulders towards the ears
    fix: Draw your shoulders back and down before you lift.
  - mistake: Clenching the buttocks hard
    fix: Keep your legs gently active without gripping.

modifications:
  - Stay very low, lifting only your head and the top of your chest.
  - Rest on your forearms instead, with your elbows under your shoulders, for a gentler shape often called Sphinx.
  - Place a folded blanket under your hips if lying on your front is uncomfortable.

Cobra is usually practised low and slow. The aim is a gentle, even curve through the upper back, not the highest lift.

If the pose feels like hard work in your lower back, come lower. Smaller is often better here.
