name: Chair Pose
sanskrit:
  iast: Utkaṭāsana
  simple: Utkatasana
alsoKnownAs: [Chair, Fierce Pose]
summary: A standing pose where you bend your knees and sit back as if lowering onto an invisible chair, warming the legs and building focus.

level: beginner
category: standing
alsoIn: []
bodyFocus: [legs, glutes, core]
goals: [strength, energy]
props: [none]

sided: false
holdGuide: 3–5 breaths to begin with. Short stays, repeated once or twice, are often easier than one long hold.
setup: Stand in Mountain Pose with your feet hip-width apart, or with your feet together if that feels steady.
steps:
  - Bring your hands together in front of your chest, or rest them on your hips.
  - As you breathe out, bend your knees and send your hips back, as if you were about to sit on a chair behind you.
  - Keep your knees pointing the same way as your toes, rather than letting them fall inwards.
  - Shift your weight back towards your heels so you could wiggle your toes.
  - Let your body lean forwards a little, keeping your back long and your ribs soft rather than flaring.
  - If it feels comfortable, raise your arms forwards and up, keeping your shoulders relaxed.
  - Stay for a few breaths, then come back up to standing.
breathCue: Breathe in to lengthen through your spine. Breathe out to settle a little lower, only as far as you can keep breathing easily.
comingOut: As you breathe in, press into your feet and straighten your legs to come back to standing. Lower your arms and rest in Mountain Pose for a few breaths.

benefits:
  - Builds strength in the legs and buttocks
  - Warms the body quickly
  - Asks the core to work to keep you steady
  - Many people find it helps them feel focused and alert

commonMistakes:
  - mistake: Letting the knees drift inwards
    fix: Keep your knees in line with your toes, and come up a little if they keep falling in.
  - mistake: Putting all the weight in the toes
    fix: Sit back further so most of your weight is in your heels.
  - mistake: Arching the lower back and flaring the ribs
    fix: Soften your ribs down and lengthen your tailbone gently towards the floor.
  - mistake: Holding for too long
    fix: Come up after a few breaths and repeat if you like, rather than straining in one long hold.

modifications:
  - Keep your hands together at your chest or on your hips instead of raising your arms.
  - Bend your knees only a little.
  - Stand with your back near a wall, or hold the back of a sturdy chair, for support.
  - Take your feet hip-width apart for a steadier base.

Chair Pose asks for effort, and it is usually felt quickly in the legs. That warmth is normal. Strain, or holding your breath, is a sign to ease off.

A small, steady version you can breathe in is more useful than a deep one you cannot.
