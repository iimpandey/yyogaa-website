name: Tree Pose
sanskrit:
  iast: Vṛkṣāsana
  simple: Vrksasana
alsoKnownAs: []
summary: A standing balance on one leg, with the sole of the other foot resting on the inner leg, practised on both sides.

level: beginner
category: balance
alsoIn: [standing]
bodyFocus: [legs, feet, core, hips]
goals: [focus, strength, posture]
props: [none]

sided: true
holdGuide: 3–8 slow breaths on each side.
setup: Stand in Mountain Pose near a wall or a sturdy chair, in case you would like to hold on.
steps:
  - Shift your weight onto your left foot and feel it press evenly into the floor.
  - Bend your right knee and turn it out to the side.
  - Place the sole of your right foot on your left ankle, lower leg or inner thigh. Avoid pressing it into the side of your knee.
  - Press your foot and your standing leg gently into each other to help you feel steady.
  - Bring your palms together in front of your chest, rest your hands on your hips, or hold the wall.
  - Rest your gaze on one still point in front of you.
  - Stay for a few breaths, then lower your foot and repeat on the other side.
breathCue: Keep your breath slow and even. If you wobble, let the out-breath help you soften and find your balance again.
comingOut: Lower your raised foot to the floor, return to Mountain Pose, and pause for a breath before the second side.

benefits:
  - Invites focus and concentration
  - Builds strength in the standing leg and foot
  - Lets you practise balance in a playful, low-pressure way

commonMistakes:
  - mistake: Pressing the foot into the side of the knee
    fix: Place your foot above or below the knee, on the inner thigh, calf or ankle.
  - mistake: Letting the standing hip sink out to the side
    fix: Gently draw the standing hip in so your pelvis stays level.
  - mistake: Holding the breath while balancing
    fix: Keep breathing slowly. A little wobble is part of the pose.
  - mistake: Letting the eyes wander
    fix: Choose one still point to rest your gaze on.

modifications:
  - Keep the toes of your raised foot on the floor, like a kickstand, with the heel resting against your ankle.
  - Stand beside a wall or a sturdy chair and hold it lightly.
  - Keep your hands on your hips rather than lifting them.
